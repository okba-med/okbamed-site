Shorts Publisher

Shorts Publisher helps a YouTube channel owner manage channel analytics, video uploads and scheduled publishing in one place.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What Shorts Publisher does

Channel analytics

Reads the performance reports of a channel you authorize (for example views, watch time, audience countries and traffic sources) to understand how videos perform and to help choose publishing times.

Video uploads

Uploads videos, with their titles, descriptions and other details, to the channel you authorize. Only videos that the channel owner has reviewed and approved are uploaded.

Scheduled publishing

Sets each approved video to be published at a chosen date and time using YouTube's own scheduling feature. YouTube then makes the video public at that time.

Permissions you are asked to grant

When you authorize a channel, Google shows you a consent screen. Shorts Publisher asks for exactly these three permissions and no others:

PermissionGoogle scopeUsed for
Read channel information and videoshttps://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonlyConfirming which channel was authorized and reading its video list and details.
Read YouTube Analytics reportshttps://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonlyReading the authorized channel's analytics reports to understand performance and plan publishing times.
Upload videoshttps://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.uploadUploading approved videos to the authorized channel, with a scheduled publish time.

You stay in control

  • You choose which channel to authorize, and each channel is authorized separately through your own Google sign-in.
  • You can withdraw access at any time at your Google Account permissions page.
  • Shorts Publisher does not request permission to edit or delete existing videos, and does not request access to earnings or revenue data.
  • A video is only scheduled for publication after the channel owner has approved that video.

Who uses Shorts Publisher

Shorts Publisher is operated by the owner of this website to manage YouTube channels that the operator owns or is authorized to manage. It is not a general-purpose service open to the public.
